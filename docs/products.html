Open-source foundations, proprietary intelligence.

We build on transparent open-source audio runtimes while keeping Vaani's custom-trained models and dialect IP protected.

Open-Source Foundations

Auditable, offline-first runtimes anyone can inspect.

  • Faster-Whisper & CTranslate2: Fast int8/float16 speech decoding on local CPU, CUDA, and NPU.
  • IndicConformer (AI4Bharat): Base Odia & Hindi acoustic CTC models.
  • OmniVoice (Apache-2.0): Base zero-shot multilingual voice synthesis runtime.

Vaani Custom & Proprietary IP

Fine-tuned weights, dialect engines, and self-learning pipelines owned by Vaani.

  • MIRA V2 & Whisper-Small-IN: Custom fine-tuned weights for Indian English, Hindi, and Odia code-mixing.
  • Bidirectional Dialect Normalizer: 5-dialect Odia normalization (Sambalpuri, Ganjami, Kalahandia, Anugulia, Baleswari).
  • Self-Learning Audio-Edit Loop: Captures user corrections + exact audio slices to retrain and improve automatically.
